## Purpose

- Members review and edit their own details.
- SUPER_ADMIN manages any member's team data: role, position, state, jersey
  number, jersey name and sizes.
- Reached from the account menu (own profile) or a
  [Roster](/docs/features/team-management/roster) row.

## Access

<PermissionMatrix resource="profile" />

- GUEST has no `view`: the proxy redirects to `/forbidden`, and
  `getUserProfile` calls `forbidden()`.
- Every other role holds `profile:edit`, so actions re-check ownership.

### `viewOnly`

UI-only flag from `getUserProfile`; disables Edit on the Personal and Team
cards and shows a **View Only** badge in the header.

- PLAYER / COACH: editable on their own profile, view only on another member.
- SUPER_ADMIN: view only on their own profile, editable on another member.

- `old_path` must be equal, not a prefix: legacy keys have a random suffix.
- `users/1` would prefix-match `users/12-abc`.
- So only the caller's current avatar is deletable.

## Layout

- One page, no tabs: a header card, then the Personal and Team cards side by
  side from `lg` (stacked below).
- Phones: the photo sits beside the name and badges, and **View performance**
  spans the card below. From `md` the header is one row.
- Every field is in view without a click. An unset one reads "Not provided".

<Tabs items={['Header', 'Personal', 'Team']}>
  <Tab value="Header">

    - Avatar (initials fallback), name, **Captain** and **View Only** badges.
    - Role, position and state badges.
    - Jersey number: a badge on phones, a large captioned number from `md`.
    - "Joined for …" from the open membership stint; the date itself is on
      the Team card.
    - **View performance** opens `/performance/<id>` on a player profile, for
      admins and the player themselves.
    - Upload overlay for the owner only (`sessionUser.id === user.id`).
    - `ImageUploader`: PNG or JPEG, up to 10 MB; stored as a 512 px WebP.
    - On success the session is refetched, so the header avatar updates.

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Personal">

    - Shows email and phone as `mailto:` / `tel:` links, date of birth with
      age, citizen ID.
    - Citizen ID always reads `********1219`: only the last 4 digits.
    - SUPER_ADMIN and the captain get an eye toggle to show the full number.
      The owner sees it in the edit form.
    - `getUserProfile` masks it on the server for everyone else, so the full
      number reaches only the owner, SUPER_ADMIN and the captain.
    - Edit: email (read-only), full name, date of birth, phone, citizen ID.
    - Save only when dirty and valid; Cancel resets.

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Team">

    - Shows role, position, state and join date (of the open membership
      stint).
    - Position is spelled out ("Power Forward", "Head Coach") from the
      `*PositionHint` messages; the header badge keeps the short "PF".
    - Player profiles add a **Jersey** section under a labelled rule: number,
      jersey name, shirt and short size, short type. Two per row while the
      card is wide enough, one per row otherwise.
    - Rows follow the profile's role, not the viewer's.
    - Edit: role and position are SUPER_ADMIN only. The Jersey section shows
      while the picked role is Player.
    - Jersey name and sizes prefill the
      [jersey order form](/docs/features/team-management/roster).
    - Saving with state `active` mutates SWR `CACHE_KEY.PLAYERS`, refreshing
      player pickers.

  </Tab>

## Rules

- **Unique jersey per team.** `isJerseyNumberTaken` (excluding the edited
  member) runs before any write.
- **Private avatar files.** WebP under `users/<user_id>/<uuid>.webp`.
- **Avatar reads.** `getAvatar` returns a base64 data URL, cached by
  `useUserAvatar` (SWR immutable).
- **Join date lives on the membership.** `join_date` is read from and written
  to the member's open `user_team` stint, not a column on `user`.
- **State changes keep the team history.** Setting state to `inactive` closes
  the open stint with today's leave date. Setting a member with no open stint
  back from `inactive` opens a new stint joined today.
- **Role switching.** `updateTeamInfo` upserts the `player` / `coach` row for
  the _submitted_ role and deletes the previous role's row.
  - User, stint and role rows are written in one transaction
    (`updateUserTeamInfo`).
- **Revalidation.** Every write calls `revalidatePath('/profile/<id>')`.

`withResource('profile')`.

## Acceptance

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Ownership is enforced without the UI">

    - A PLAYER calling updatePersonalInfo or updateTeamInfo for another
      user_id is rejected.
    - So is their own user_id with a changed role. Nothing is written.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Admin edits others, not self">

    - Another member: Role, Position, State, Jersey Number, Jersey Name,
      Shirt Size and Short Size are editable.
    - Own profile: both cards view only; the avatar can still be replaced.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Avatar limits and replacement">

    - Over 10 MB or not PNG/JPEG: rejected client-side, and again on the server.
    - Valid upload: updates the avatar, deletes the old file, refreshes the
      session.
    - old_path not equal to the stored image: rejected, nothing deleted.

  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
