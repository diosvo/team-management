## Purpose

- Reference list of opponents (`is_external = true`): name, contact email,
  founding year, logo.
- Picked as the home or away side when a match is recorded.
- The club's own team (`is_default = true`) is never external, so it never
  shows here and can't be edited here.

## Access

<PermissionMatrix resource="teams" />

- PLAYER, COACH and SUPER_ADMIN view.
- Only SUPER_ADMIN creates, edits, uploads logos or deletes.
- Row click is bound to `can('teams', 'edit')`; viewers never open the dialog.
- GUEST has no `view`; redirected to `/forbidden`.

## Flows

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Browse

    - Sorted by name; `q` searches name and email.
    - The name cell shows the logo as a rounded avatar, initials as fallback.
    - `useTeamLogo` fetches via `getLogo`; SWR-cached by storage path.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Add or update

    - **+ Add** and row click open the same dialog: name, email, establish year.
    - **Add** stays open and resets the form; **Update** closes it.
    - Saving revalidates `/teams` and the `opponents` SWR key (match form).

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Upload a logo

    - Only in **Update**, because the blob path is `teams/{team_id}`.
    - A PNG or JPEG under 100 KB calls `uploadLogo` at once, apart from Save.
    - Other files are rejected client-side with a toast.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Delete

    - Select rows and delete.
    - `removeTeam` runs per id; the toast lists any failure message.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules

- **The default team is untouchable.**
  - `insertTeam` forces `is_default: false` and `is_external: true`.
  - `updateTeam` / `deleteTeam` add `is_external = true` to `WHERE`; a forged
    `team_id` is a no-op.
  - Partial unique index `default_team` allows one default row.
  - Check `default_team_not_external` forbids a team being both.
- **Validation** (`UpsertTeamSchema`):
  - Name 3–128 characters.
  - Email optional, valid, ≤ 128 characters, unique across teams (DB).
  - Establish year: integer 2000 to current year (default), also a `CHECK`.
- **Logos are private blobs.**
  - `uploadFile` calls `@vercel/blob` `put` with `access: 'private'`,
    `addRandomSuffix: true`.
  - The row stores the returned `pathname` in `image`.
  - `getLogo` reads it server-side as a base64 data URL; no public URL.
  - The previous blob is deleted after the row updates.
- **Teams in a match cannot be deleted.**
  - `match.home_team` / `away_team` are `ON DELETE restrict`.
  - `removeTeam` counts those matches first and returns a plain message: "This
    team appears in N match(es). Delete those matches first, or keep the team
    to preserve the record."
- **Cascades.** League entries of a deleted team go with it; external teams
  carry no members.

## Acceptance

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Viewers see the list without controls">
    A PLAYER or COACH sees every opponent; no **+ Add**, no checkboxes, rows
    do nothing on click.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A duplicate email is rejected">
    An email already used by another team fails with an error toast.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A team with matches is kept">
    Deleting a team that played a recorded match shows the match count in an
    error toast; the team stays.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Logo upload is validated">

    - PNG/JPEG under 100 KB replaces the avatar and removes the old blob.
    - A larger or non-image file shows an error toast; the logo is unchanged.

  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
