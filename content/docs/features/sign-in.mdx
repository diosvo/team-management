## Purpose

- The only pages reachable without a session.
- No sign-up: accounts are created from Roster, then activated via
  `/new-password`.
- This page covers the screens; the end-to-end flow is in
  [Authentication](/docs/user-flows/authentication).

## Access

- No resource or permission check; all three are in `PUBLIC_ROUTES`.
- **Signed in:** the proxy redirects any auth route, and `/`, to `/dashboard`.
- **Signed out:** every other route redirects to `/login`.

## Flows

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Sign in (`/login`)

    - Email + password, validated by `LoginSchema`.
    - **Sign In** stays disabled until the form is dirty and valid.
    - Calls `authClient.signIn.email` with `callbackURL: '/dashboard'`.
    - **Forgot your password?** links to `/forgot-password`.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Request a reset link (`/forgot-password`)

    - One email field (`ForgotPasswordSchema`).
    - Calls `authClient.requestPasswordReset` with `redirectTo: '/new-password'`.
    - Success: clears the form and shows "Password reset instructions have been
      sent to your email."

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Set a new password (`/new-password`)

    - Reads `token` from the URL; the reset email links here.
    - A checklist turns green as each password rule passes.
    - **Submit** stays disabled until all four rules pass.
    - Success: toast, then `router.push('/login')`.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules

- **Password rules** (`/new-password` only):
  - 8–128 characters.
  - At least one letter, one number and one special character.
- **Login validation.** `LoginSchema` checks the email and an 8–128 character
  password, not the character rules.
- **Rate limit.** Sign-in, reset request and reset are each capped at 3
  requests/min per IP (`STRICT_RATE_LIMIT` in `src/lib/auth.ts`).
- **Error messages** come from `authErrorMessage` (`src/utils/rate-limit.ts`):
  - `429` → `Rate limit exceeded. Retry at <time>`, from `Retry-After`.
  - Anything else → Better Auth's message, or "Something went wrong."
- **Rejected credentials** (`401`) keep **Sign In** disabled until the email
  or password is edited.
- **Network failure** on login shows "Unable to reach the server. Please try
  again."
- **Signed-in users can't reset.** `/new-password` is an auth route, so a
  reset link opened while signed in lands on `/dashboard`.

## Acceptance

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Wrong password blocks retry until edited">
    After a rejected sign-in, **Sign In** stays disabled until a field changes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fourth attempt in a minute is rate-limited">
    The error shows the time at which a retry is allowed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Weak password cannot be submitted">
    `abcdefgh` leaves the number and special-character rules unticked and
    **Submit** disabled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Signed-in users skip the auth pages">
    Opening `/login` with a valid session redirects to `/dashboard`.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
