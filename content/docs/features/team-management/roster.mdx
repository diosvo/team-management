## Purpose

- Single list of who is on the team: role, state and jersey number.
- Admins and captains manage membership here.
- Admins and captains also build the jersey order form here.
- Personal details are edited on the member's [profile](/docs/features/profile).

## Access

<PermissionMatrix resource="roster" />

- Every role can view.
- Captains inherit PLAYER plus full management: they invite and remove like a
  SUPER_ADMIN.
- **Jersey form** shows for SUPER_ADMIN and captains only (`isAdmin ||
isCaptain`), separate from the `roster:create` check on **+ Add**.

## Flows

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Invite

    - **+ Add** opens the invite dialog.
    - `createUser` creates the account (temporary password) and the `player` or
      `coach` row.
    - It also opens a `user_team` stint from the join date, or the club's
      founding date (`ESTABLISHED_DATE`) when none is given.
    - Resend sends a "Create a new password" email.
    - The invitee activates at `/new-password`; no separate verification step.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Filter

    - Search by name or email; filter by role and state.
    - Filters live in the URL: shareable and kept on reload.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Order jerseys

    - **Jersey form** opens a dialog: uniform provider (Hoops, Versus, Others)
      and the default price per set.
    - **Download** builds `[Rovers] Đặt đồ - <year>.xlsx` in the browser.
      - exceljs loads only on click.
    - **Open in Google Sheets** opens a new tab straight from the click.
      - The tab signs in with Google (`drive.file` scope, full-page redirect).
      - It waits on `/google-callback` while the dialog uploads the file.
      - It then lands on the new Google Sheet in the user's Drive.
      - The link is shared as "anyone with the link can edit", so players fill
        in their own rows; a warning toast says when sharing fails.
      - Within the hour, the token is reused and sign-in is skipped.
    - The sheet is remembered on the team
      (`team.uniform_order_spreadsheet_id`) for the current year.
      - Next time, the dialog shows when it was created and **Open sheet**
        links straight to it.
      - **Create new** makes a fresh sheet and replaces the remembered link;
        the old one stays in Drive.
    - Only shows when `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` is set; see
      [Deployment](/docs/architecture/deployment).

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Remove

    - Select rows and delete in bulk.
    - Deleting a member also removes their membership history.
    - A summary toast reports the outcome.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules

- **Narrow projection.** The list reads `RosterUser` only.
  - Date of birth, phone and citizen ID never cross the Server Component
    boundary; the profile page shows them under its own ownership rules.
- **Current members only.** The list filters on `user.team_id`, the pointer
  to each member's open `user_team` stint.
- **Jersey numbers are unique per team.** Checked by `isJerseyNumberTaken` in
  the action, not the DB, since `player` reaches its team through `user`.
  - Set on the profile's Team tab; the invite form has no jersey number.
  - Duplicates only exist in the jersey order spreadsheet, never in the app.
- **SUPER_ADMIN rows are excluded** from the list.
- **Deep-linkable state.** `q`, `role` and `state` are URL query params.

### Jersey order form

Built by `src/app/(protected)/(team-management)/roster/_helpers/jersey-order.ts`.

- **Tabs.**
  - **Đội**: active players sorted by number, plus 5 spare rows.
  - **Ngoài đội**: 30 empty rows for fans and friends.
  - **Bảng size**: the provider's size charts.
  - **Mẫu đồ**: left empty.
- **Columns.** STT, Tên, Số áo, Tên trên áo, Size áo, Size quần, Loại quần,
  Đã thu tiền, Note.
  - Name, number, jersey name, both sizes and Loại quần come from each
    player's [profile](/docs/features/profile).
- **Dropdowns.** Sizes XS–4XL; Loại quần is Form ngắn or Form tiêu chuẩn.
  - Each column gets one range validation, so Google Sheets shows them as
    chips.
- **Duplicate numbers.** On **Đội** only, a repeated Số áo turns the name and
  number light orange.
  - The rule recalculates on every edit: once one player changes, the other
    row clears.
  - A banner asks players to agree and update later. **Ngoài đội** allows
    repeats.
- **Paid.** Đã thu tiền holds TRUE/FALSE, unticked by default; ticked turns
  green.
  - Google Sheets: real checkboxes, added after upload through the Sheets API.
  - Google Sheets: the column is locked so only the team's super admins and
    captains (and the owner) can tick it; anyone else with the link sees it
    read-only.
  - Excel downloads can't lock by email, so the column stays editable there.
  - Excel: a TRUE/FALSE dropdown, since exceljs can't write checkbox cells.
  - Without the Sheets API the sheet still opens, with a warning toast.
- **Fixed price.** No price per row. A summary on **Đội** shows Đơn giá, Số
  bộ (named rows on both tabs), Tổng tiền đồ, Đã thu (ticked × price) and
  Còn lại.
- **Size charts.** Images from `assets/providers/<provider>/`, re-encoded from
  WebP to PNG, each under an uppercase title.
  - Hoops: SIZE ĐỒ, SIZE QUẦN NGẮN, SIZE QUẦN DÀI.
  - Providers without images get a note instead.
- **Styling.** Arial 10 throughout; headers copy the club's sheet colours.

## Acceptance

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Viewers see the list without controls">
    GUEST, non-captain PLAYER and COACH see every member, but no **+ Add** or
    selection checkboxes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A duplicate jersey number is rejected">
    Saving a member's profile with a jersey number used on the team fails:
    “Jersey number `<n>` is already taken”.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Only admins and captains order jerseys">
    **Jersey form** is hidden from every other member.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Duplicate numbers are flagged in the form">
    Two players on **Đội** with the same Số áo both turn light orange; fixing
    one clears both.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A second export reopens the same sheet">
    Once this year's sheet exists, **Open sheet** links to it instead of
    creating another; **Create new** replaces it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Google Sheets opens in one tab">
    **Open in Google Sheets** opens one tab that ends on the new sheet, with
    Đã thu tiền as checkboxes and a link anyone can edit. No pop-up and no
    extra click.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Personal data stays server-side">
    The page payload has no date of birth, phone number or citizen ID.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Activation email">
    A successful invite sends a “Create a new password” email linking to
    `/new-password`.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
