## Purpose

- Single list of who is on the team: role, state and jersey number.
- Admins and captains manage membership here.
- Personal details are edited on the member's [profile](/docs/features/profile).

## Access

<PermissionMatrix resource="roster" />

- Every role can view.
- Captains inherit PLAYER plus full management: they invite and remove like a
  SUPER_ADMIN.

## Flows

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Invite

    - **+ Add** opens the invite dialog.
    - `addUser` creates the account (temporary password) and the `player` or
      `coach` row.
    - It also opens a `user_team` stint from the join date, or the club's
      founding date (`ESTABLISHED_DATE`) when none is given.
    - Resend sends a "Create a new password" email.
    - The invitee activates at `/new-password`; no separate verification step.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Filter

    - Search by name or email; filter by role and state.
    - Filters live in the URL: shareable and kept on reload.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Remove

    - Select rows and delete in bulk.
    - Deleting a member also removes their membership history.
    - A summary toast reports the outcome.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules

- **Narrow projection.** The list reads `RosterUser` only.
  - Date of birth, phone and citizen ID never cross the Server Component
    boundary; the profile page shows them under its own ownership rules.
- **Current members only.** The list filters on `user.team_id`, the pointer
  to each member's open `user_team` stint.
- **Jersey numbers are unique per team.** Checked by `isJerseyNumberTaken` in
  the action, not the DB, since `player` reaches its team through `user`.
  - Set on the profile's Team tab; the invite form has no jersey number.
- **SUPER_ADMIN rows are excluded** from the list.
- **Deep-linkable state.** `q`, `role` and `state` are URL query params.

## Acceptance

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Viewers see the list without controls">
    GUEST, non-captain PLAYER and COACH see every member, but no **+ Add** or
    selection checkboxes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A duplicate jersey number is rejected">
    Saving a member's profile with a jersey number used on the team fails:
    “Jersey number `<n>` is already taken”.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Personal data stays server-side">
    The page payload has no date of birth, phone number or citizen ID.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Activation email">
    A successful invite sends a “Create a new password” email linking to
    `/new-password`.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
