Team Management is a web app for managing a basketball club's teams, people,
and operations (rosters, schedules, assets) with role-based access. One
private, role-based hub that is the club's single source of truth.

This section covers the strategic top of the PRD:

- [Vision](/docs/overview/vision) — the problem, the vision, non-goals, and constraints.
- [Goals](/docs/overview/goals) — goals, success metrics, and non-functional requirements.
- [Personas](/docs/overview/personas) — target users and their jobs to be done.
- [Principles](/docs/overview/principles) — shared UX expectations and cross-feature requirements.

## Primary use cases (jobs to be done)

- A player checks team info, training schedule, and performance metrics.
- A coach manages training sessions, attendance, and match records.
- A captain handles registration, manages the roster, and edits team rules.
- An admin manages teams, configuration, and all operational data.
