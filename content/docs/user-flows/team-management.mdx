## From roster to match day

<Mermaid
  chart={`
flowchart TD
    subgraph CA["Captain / Admin"]
        R[Roster: invite, remove] --> REG[Registration: PDF / CSV for a league]
    end
    subgraph AD["Admin"]
        LG[Leagues: enrol team, pick players] --> REG
    end
    subgraph CO["Coach"]
        T[Training: schedule] --> A[Attendance: per session]
        M[Matches: schedule, then results]
        PT[Periodic Testing]
    end
    R --> T
    LG --> M
    A --> D[Dashboard]
    M --> D
    PT --> D
`}
/>

- **Roster** (`addUser`, `removeUser`, gated by `roster`): where a member
  starts; every other page reads `user` / `player` from it.
- **Leagues** (`upsertLeague`, admin only): enrols the caller's team via
  `league_team`; syncs picked players into `league_team_roster` (persisted).
- **Registration** (`registration:create`, captain and admin): fills an
  optional PDF template's AcroForm from active players and leagues.
- Exports PDF or CSV; saved registrations live in `localStorage`, not the DB.
- **Matches** (`upsertMatch`, gated by `matches`): home is the caller's team,
  away an external team, plus optional `league_id`; none means a friendly.
- A match starts `scheduled` and feeds **Upcoming Matches**; once `completed`
  with a score it counts toward Dashboard win rate and results.
- A team that appears in any match can't be deleted.

## Training and attendance

<Mermaid
  chart={`
sequenceDiagram
    autonumber
    actor Coach

    participant App

    Coach->>App: upsertSession (training:create)
    Note over App: fails without a team head coach<br/>new sessions get coach_id = head coach
    App-->>Player: Session appears in Training
    opt cannot attend
        Player->>App: submitLeave (attendance:create, own player_id only)
    end
    Coach->>App: updateStatus per player (attendance:edit)
    App->>App: revalidate attendances → dashboard rate and reasons

`}
/>

Database constraints:

- One record per `(player, session)`.
- Leave request: `session_id = null`, unique per `(player, date)`.
- Rescheduling a session cascades the new `date` into its attendance rows.
